A strange man wearing torn rags that helped the party defeat the Bhaalists at Blackstone Ravine. He spoke little, preferring to let his fists do the talking, and barely interacted with the party once the last abomination was dealt with. The short talk with him while he was taking the bottom jaws of the fallen cultists revealed little information beyond him assuring them her was a friend and that he would deal with more nearby Bhaalists while the party helped the refugees escape.

He also confirmed he was the one that left the note and silver scales at Flemington Planes, and seemed to express sadness at not being able to help them more, though he did not elaborate further.

As he was leaving, he looked at Kaldoris with an expression almost of recognition and cryptically said:

"Good to see you've made allies... Remember not to trust his lies."
