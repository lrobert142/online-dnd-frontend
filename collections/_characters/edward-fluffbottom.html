An adorable bundle of energy with an insatiable hunger for knowledge, Edward came to Phandalin in hopes of uncovering the location of the Phandelver Mine! Of course, now that it's been found he has turned his attention to other things and asked Kaldoris to allow him to build a library in Thundertree. He promises to collect knowledge from all over and make it worth everyone's while!

"Just because one ancient mystery has been solved doesn't mean there aren't more to uncover! Why, I heard a rumour about a banshee trapped in a well, can you believe it?!"

After hearing about his friends moving from the ruins of Thundertree to the small settlement of Wolfhaven, he jumped at the opportunity to explore a new area and continue his research. Especially now that he has a fully furnished library at his disposal!
