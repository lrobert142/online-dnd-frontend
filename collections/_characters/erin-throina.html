Though a devout priestess of Waukeen, she believes in supporting all kinds of religious beliefs and helping guide others through difficult times. Upon hearing that the party was planning to establish roots in Thundertree, she asked Gaelin to be allowed to establish a place of worship there to give others hope in times of need.

"Religion and belief is strong, it brings the gods protection to us and makes us strong. With the god's watching over us, we will make Thundertree a safe and kind place."

When The Lord's Alliance arrived at Wolfhaven seeking information about the party's whereabouts, they risked stumbling upon the lycanthropes and uncovering something far more difficult to explain away. However, with quick thinking and a kindly guiding voice, Erin was able to convince the guardsmen not to enter the forest by proclaiming it to be a sanctuary dedicated to Silvanus. By offering them a small bone statue as a token of Silvanus' appreciation (carved by a lycanthrope, unbeknownst to the guardsmen) she was able to seal the deal and keep everyone out of trouble.
