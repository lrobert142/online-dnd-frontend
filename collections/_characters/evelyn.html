A young goblin lady who claims to have been a quite successful tinkerer before she was captured and enslaved. After being freed by the party from her forced servitude she was incredibly thankful and has promised to build and run a workshop for Sýnc to use in Thundertree (and also use it herself to keep tinkering).

"No explosives this time though, mistakes like that end up costing you an arm and a leg! Well, just a leg in my case."

Being told to pack up and move once again from Thundertree to Wolfhaven was a bother that Evelyn kept mostly to herself, quiet mumbles about having to be more careful about blowing things up now that she's no longer in stone ruins. However, upon seeing the solid construction of her new workshop she nearly immediately perked right up and now loves her new home!
