Head priest of Ilmater's Mercy Church, he is kind, gentle, understanding, and never harshly judges another. He seeks to embody the goodness that Ilmater promises and has unbreakable loyalty to his god. Still, he does let his faith get in the way of others beliefs and will honour their own gods - or lack thereof if they are not religious - provided they show proper respect while in the church itself.

Upon seeing the gryphon plush in the party's care, the father felt the immense Divine power emanating from it and was able to discern it as an artefact of Ilmater! Using a powerful ritual, they we able to Commune with Ilmater to attempt to glean understanding into what was happening, how the god was interacting through the Blackout, and why he had imbued a child's toy with such power! Alas, as the ritual started they immediately felt it break down and a wave of incredible fatigue wash over them.

Through the party's explanation of stopped time and messages from Ilmater himself, Father Myron was glad he could have been of at least some assistance by admitted he may not be able to do much more for quite some time until his strength fully recovers.
