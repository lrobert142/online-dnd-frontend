Also known as The Broken God and The One Who Endures, Ilmater's domain is the protection of the suffering, the oppressed, and the persecuted. He endures the pain inflicted upon others to alleviate them from their burdens and ensure they live safe and painless lives. Being the patron god of Malgalus, he is widely revered to be a beacon of hope and kindness, and many aspire to emulate his selflessness.

His holy symbol is a pair clasped of marble hands, bound in thick red rope, symbolising his willingness to be bound in pain to alleviate the suffering of others.

Through the power of the Blessed Gryphon Plush divine artefact and a Commune ritual conducted by Father Myron, Ilmater was able to somehow circumvent the Malgalus Blackout and temporarily speak with the party. Though it seemed to cause significant pain to do so.
