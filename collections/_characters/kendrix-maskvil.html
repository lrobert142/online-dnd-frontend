Owner of The Blazing Glory in Redorahh, they are a masterful blacksmith able to even imbue a small portion of magic into their weapons enhancing their abilities beyond what many others can. Yet their talent does not go to their head, and he has a genuinely excitable, happy demeanour. Thoroughly enjoys working on new and interesting projects, especially if said projects involve unusual materials.

He is deathly afraid of water, and seems to have a particular dislike of hydras.

After encountering him at his forge late at night, the party was able to persuade him into selling them a +1 Greatsword after Petee's previous greatsword had been "lost while fighting a hydra". Their tales filled his heart with determination and he was more than happy to part with this valuable and powerful weapon. As long as they promise to use it to slay any other hydra they come across.
