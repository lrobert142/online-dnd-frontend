One of the captains of the paladins of Redorahh, and second-in-command serving under Sir Anima, Marielle serves the city with a work ethic that puts many others to shame. She has frequently shown herself to be the first to jump into action when situations grow dire, always one to stand on the front-line to defend from outside attacks, and is widely loved by both the people and other guards. Even other captains look up to her as a shining beacon of what a guard should be.

She is also surprisingly calm given wolverines are generally known to have short tempers and a vicious disposition as well as a love of fighting. When asked why she simply shrugged her shoulders and said she was born different. “The world has enough fighting already, why not do something better for it?”

Disaster strikes! Sir Anima has been identified as a traitor and intends to resurrect a lich in the newly uncovered Xibal Crypt to the north. Though she cannot spare many resources, she beseeches the party to track him down and bring an end to his treachery! She will stay behind and make sure that Milo and her can safely look after the refugees, making sure none of his corruption can reach them.

She apologises for the early morning wakeup, but explains that if that lich were to fall under Bhaalist control, it would cause destruction far beyond comprehension. It is imperative they help her paladins finish their ritual so Anima does not succeed!

Those who dabble with traps must be wary, for traps are known to spring on those who set them.

Marielle's ploy has been uncovered and she was captured while attempting to 'visit' the new refugees. Though she initially tried to convince the party that she was innocent and Sir Anima was manipulating them, when she was confronted by her rat underling her demeanour unravelled and she revealed her true colours.

Spitting venomous taunts in every word, she belittled the party for believing her and revealed that she was a Bhaalist leader and had been leading refugees to the slaughter. The maddened look in her eyes showed how vile she really is as she was as she taunted the party about being unable to save the current people she had sent to their deaths.

With a final insane laugh, she was dragged away into the Redorahh prisons by Sir Anima to await a proper trial - and likely execution - back in Ithacos. Surrounded by guards every waking moment, she knows now that she will never escape. And yet, he twisted faith in Bhaal and his reign of terror never seems to falter.
