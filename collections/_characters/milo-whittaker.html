A cocky, arrogant, somewhat angry young paladin. He seems to dislike the party and Sir Anima, though refuses to say why. His brash personality has earned him few friends.

After being alerted to supplies being stolen from the church, the party devised a stakeout and was able to catch the thief in the act. Pulling off his hood it was revealed that it was none other than Milo! After a back and forth verbal joust, he eventually revealed he was only stealing the supplies to give to refugees as the paladins of Redorahh were too corrupt under Sir Anima's influence to actually help them.

Though his words were sharp and his tone distrusting, he did lead the party to refugees hidden under an abandoned house. Half-starved, injured, and in poor health, but alive. He revealed that he had to hide them away due to a letter he'd received from Sir Anima indicating that Redorahh could not support refugees and would be ejected without hesitation. Milo could not bear to put others in danger like that, and instead secreted them away here into safety.

The letter revelation left the party confused and angry, how could something like this happen?

The reveal that Sir Anima was not the monster after all and that Marielle had been using him to heard refugees to the slaughter - and was the one responsible for his hometown not receiving reinforcements in him - weighs heavily upon Milo's mind. He only meant the best and now feels the crushing weight of what he had inadvertently partaken in. In a desire to make atonement, he agreed to protect the refugees and lead Marielle into a trap.

Though it was successful, he still feels his actions pulling him down and weighing heavily on his mind. Will he be able to overcome this? Does he deserve to?

With Milo's help, the party was able to catch Marielle in the act and keep the refugees safe. Though it pained his heart to know she had stolen them away for a cruel ritual, something changed within his heart and instead of despair he felt a sense of duty to look after those he still had under his care and work ever harder to atone for his 'sins'. He promises to keep the remaining refugees safe, no matter what!

To say Milo was excited to see all the refugees back safe and sound with the party would have been the understatement of the century. His excitement was matched only by his admiration for the party, and he promised once again to do everything possible to look after the refugees; feed them, clothe them, and work tirelessly to see them back to full strength.

His actions and commitment to the greater good did not go unnoticed, and after making amends with Sir Anima, he was able to enrol as a paladin as well. Though his training will take many years yet, he remains resolute to be the best he can be and protect the innocent until his dying breath.
