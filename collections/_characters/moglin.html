A young goblin barely out of his teens who struggles greatly with social interactions. A real bundle of nerves, he is very adept at dealing with plants and after being graciously saved by the party from a lifetime of slavery has promised to raise a garden in Sýnc's honor.

"I never was very good at mining, but I know plants! I-I could grow you some real nice things, I think. I-I-If that'd be OK with you. I don't mean to impose."

Being asked to move from more stone ruins to a proper village of people left Moglin feeling both excited and terrified. Excited at the prospect of seeing a new location that wasn't just a crumbling mess, but terrified of how people might view him as a goblin. His fears were alleviated when the townsfolk took a liking to him and he found himself oddly enamoured by the lycanthropes of the strange village.

Finding a lost dog along the road, Moglin nursed the mutt back to health and adopted her. He calls her Rumbletum, and the two are inseparable.
