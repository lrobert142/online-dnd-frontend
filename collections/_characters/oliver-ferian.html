Owner of the Green Thumb Apothecary in Redorahh. A lover of plants of all kinds, they have a near-encyclopedic knowledge about all sorts of herbs, roots, tree, and all things plants. He has a self-admitted green thumb (pun intended, he assures you) and enjoys growing and selling all kinds, both living plants and dried herbs that people can use; be they for adventurers or cooks. He is also a *budding* alchemist (pun also intended) and can make some simple potions though is far from an expert so more complex items are admittedly beyond his ability.

Above all, he wants to work with plants from other planes and would give nearly anything to have the opportunity to grow them in his garden and see them flourish.

Upon seeing the Spoilseed potion, Oliver was quite upset and distraught; a vile and cruel potion like that should not exist and most definitely be destroyed! After hearing the party's need for it to confirm corruption in the city, Oliver begrudgingly allowed them to keep it, but strongly stressed that under no circumstances were they to allow it to get on their skin. They also informed the party of the best way to find those already afflicted by its vile mind control powers: Look for black veins along the arms.

Being given the Spoilseed potion to destroy - and learning that not a drop had been spilled - Oliver was only too happy to also help the party understand what the potion was that the twisted abomination of a bhaalist had drank. Sensing terrible magic and foul corruption in it, they decided to take extra precautions and only study it in their lab. A few days is all it should take to discern what it is, and perhaps who - or what - made it.
