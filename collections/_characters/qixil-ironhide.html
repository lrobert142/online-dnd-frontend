An young, full-hearted, jovial lizardfolk that grew up in a distant town in Malgalus before settling down on the outskirts of Redorahh. He acts as a freelance adventurer who seems to adore dealing with bandits, marauders, and the like, so much so that he doesn't even ask for rewards for those he dispatches. Even those with bounties on their heads he does not claim more than he needs to survive his relatively simple life, insisting that being allowed to deal with “the bad guys” is all the payment he needs. He is known around town as having a dependable reputation and being a charitable soul.

His peculiar talent for tracking across large distances are quietly revered as beyond impressive, though he himself insists its just a matter of training, training, and a lot of luck. This talent and his love of helping others has earned him the quiet respect of Sir Anima, which he absolutely leans into, ribbing his dear friend at any opportunity. Of course, he takes as good as he gives and is never one to shy away from a teasing or joke at his expense too.

Coming to the party at the behest of Sir Anima - of "that big ol' grumpy cat" as he endearingly referred to him as - he was able to use the gryphon plush to track the location of the missing refugees to a nearby location called Blackstone Ravine. He also offered to accompany them to in their journey, watching their backs and making sure everyone was safe.

Having him tag along turned out to be quite worthwhile as during the night as the party approached, they were nearly ambushed by a wandering party of Bhaalists and their enslaved elemental. However, with his quick thinking he was able to alert the party and get them in position to reverse the ambush and quickly dispatch their would-be attackers.

Sensing many other Bhaalists around them, Qixil suggested he act as a decoy, loudly announcing his position and dragging them away from the party so they could sneak in, deal with the Bhaalists, save the refugees, and meet up again after all was said and done.

"And don't worry, I'm gonna be real sneaky about it. No hero plays from me, just a distraction to buy you some time and then I'm dipping. Promise!"
