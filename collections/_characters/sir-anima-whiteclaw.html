An older gentleman who has spent many decades protecting Ithacos as one of their most decorated guard commanders (a rank above typical guard captains). His mere presence commands respect and pride from all those who serve under and alongside him, and his level head in even the most difficult and dangerous situations has saved many lives.

His age has afforded him much wisdom, but make no mistake he is still very strong and sturdy and is able to take blows that would knock out those half his age.

Called from the capital to check on Redorahh before it shut itself away, he looks forward to the day he can return to Ithacos to see his wife, daughter, and grandson again. Though he rarely speaks of them, when he does it is obvious that he admires them greatly and misses them even more.

Thanks to some shared friends he was temporarily required at the Malgalus-Andoril border where the party met him and was able to convince him they were allies with a strange, large glass diamond. Though he did not elaborate on what it meant - or why he seemed so surprised to see it "after all these decades" - it is clear it is quite significant to him.

He now travels with the party, taking them to Redorahh where they can help fight back against the Bhaalist menace tormenting Malgalus.

Returning to town after a strange message from the party, Sir Anima learned of Marielle's betrayal. Though she had tried to pin him as a monster intent on resurrecting a lich he revealed that he had been sent back to Stalerock Fort at Marielle's behest to stop a bhaalist invasion. Trusting the party, he allowed them to lead Marielle into a trap to capture and interrogate her, calling off guards that nearly captured the party after things went loud. She seemed remorseless and only broke after her rat lackey sold her out.

He is disgusted that someone as respected as her could be so rotten to the core.

With Marielle locked up, and a retinue of trusted guards watching her at all time, he turns his attention towards helping the party find the missing refugees. To that end, he called upon the help of a friend, Qixil. Though exhaustion make him unable to travel with them, he assured the party Qixil could be trusted and he would make certain he was ready to receive the rescued refugees when they returned.


As the party approached the gates of Redorahh with all kidnapped refugees in tow, Sir Anima could not hope but to smile at the sight. His worries over the ritual, the bhaalists, the party's well-being and the safety of the refugees was lifted from his shoulders and he once again felt as though he could breathe. He trust them, of course, and deep down he knew they would never fail, but seeing everyone back safe and sound helped in more ways than he could ever put into words.

He is willing to do whatever it takes to assist the party in ending this Bhaalist threat once and for all!
