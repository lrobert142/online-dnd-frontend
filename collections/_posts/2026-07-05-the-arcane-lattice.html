A Study of the Great Sundering

By Ar'azim Bi'cu'thum, circa 7512ATS

The Arcane Lattice is a fascinating, mysterious, and largely unknown phenomenon which seems to encase all planes; from the outer reaches of the Abyss to the very center of the Material Plane. It has been studied quite extensively by innumerable scholars of both Arcane, Divine, and even Nature persuasion, and yet still remains quite the oddity with many uncertainties still being discovered and studied. It is hoped that one day we might understand - and perhaps even harness - the strange and unique properties of the Arcane Lattice for the betterment of all.

A misnomer

Despite the name of the lattice, it must be noted that the Arcane Lattice does not seem to have any relation to Arcane Magic. Much as it limits Divine Magic (discussed below) it also seems to filter Arcane Magic as well, and preliminary experiments into understanding this great phenomenon seem to indicate that the Lattice is made up of something entirely different and unknown, not like any kind of magic currently in any plane of existence.

It seems the misnomer of a name traces its origins back to the early years after The Great Sundering - reportedly as early as 4ATS - when Divine Magisters found themselves unable to embody their gods and believed Arcane Magisters were disrupting their attempts with a strange new barrier. In time the truth was learned that they also were affected by, and not in control of, the Lattice. However, by that time the misnomer had already become commonplace and even now is referred to as such.

Effects of the Arcane Lattice

The Arcane Lattice affects all streams of magic: Divine, Arcane, and even Nature. How it affects their magic though seems to differ depending on the type.

Divine

Before the Arcane Lattice arose, Divine Magisters were able to undertake complex rituals to call their gods into a plane of existence. The physical embodiment of these creatures of unfathomable power - as indeed they were the gods themselves and not just their much weaker avatars as can rarely be seen today - were a sight to behold and feared. With the power to wipe out entire towns with a simple thought, or raze entire battlefields with a single spell, those who utilised Divine Magic were feared as terrors and tyrants, to be worshipped under threat of complete annihilation.

A few remaining fragments of tablets from Before the Sundering indicate at least one battle where Bahamut himself was able to enter the Material Plane, and with a single breath attack erased an entire civilization of “heretics”.

Come morn, naught was left of the heretics but ash and dust. So decreed the platinum god.

It is believed that the only reason Divine magic had not completely taken over or destroyed planes is due to the infighting of different gods and followers. Multiple stories have been recovered that indicate impossible-scale battles of rival gods that left large portions of planes devastated while each side warred with the other for land, resources, or purely to kill each other. The bloody and deadly conflicts are perhaps the only reason other magic strains remain even today, as a focused attack from the gods would surely have wiped all else from existence.

It is therefore unsurprising that the Arcane Lattice had the most devastating effect on Divine Magisters, as it completely shut out their direct interference in planar issues. No longer were they able to directly materialise in planes and use their great and terrible powers. While they still are able to filter small parts of their magic through the Lattice to clerics, paladins, and the like, the magic channelled today is a shallow reflection of what once was. Even modern Divine Interventions result in little more than miniscule fragments of power - known as avatars - being granted for mere fractions of a second.

Arcane

Arcane Magisters of old were dangerous, powerful, deadly, and incredibly volatile. They were able to pull at the Weave directly and manipulate magic in ways that are completely impossible today. So great was their power, that many of the most practised Arcane Magisters were said to have limitless access to magic, able to cast innumerable spells without fatigue or needing to rest. One such ancient account speaks of a particularly powerful witch who single-handedly destroyed a Divine capital during the war by “pulling the stars from the heavens again and again until nothing was left but burning craters and death.” Further analysis of records from the time indicate this may have been repeated usage of the spell now known as Meteor Swarm.

While these accounts are terrible and dangerous, they do nothing to recount just how powerful even the lowliest of Arcane Magisters were, with spells such as Danse Macabre able to raise not just multiple bodies as mindless undead, but entire legions of the fallen as undying soldiers with the ability to think and act independently of the caster, and even remain should the caster somehow be killed. Such powerful and potent magic are in large part why Arcane Magisters stood a chance against Divine Magisters during the Great War; death was rarely final, and the esoteric nature of their magic allowed them to enact backups upon backups should they somehow be caught out.

Compared to modern times, Arcane Magic is woefully limited in capacity and is believed to be due to magic having to be filtered through the Lattice first. This limits the amount of magic an individual may house within them, greatly reduces the effectiveness of many spells, and even outright prevents others from being cast at all as the magic simply cannot be channelled in any meaningful way. One such example is an old ritual known as Death Malaise that, as of writing, has never been able to be cast, even with several Archmagisters working tirelessly for months.

Modern Arcane Magisters are also able to hold only a limited amount of magic each day and require a rest and recovery period between bouts of powerful magic. This strange limitation of magic is still being researched, but is theorised to be due to the Lattice not allowing 'stale' magic to flow back out from an individual without significant downtime.

Nature

Unfortunately, very little is known about Nature Magic Before the Sundering, and what scraps of history have been uncovered are woefully incomplete. This is believed to be due to Nature Magisters being far more removed from many of the affairs of old and taking a neutral stance during the Great War. Their hesitance to fight and isolationist way of life leaves much unknown, but what has been found indicates their powers were easily amongst the strongest, able to create short-lived planes of existence (not to be confused with modern demi-planes, which are mere shadows of the greatness they could create). These planes supposedly acted as safe havens for small communities and could even stand against the intrusion of gods. However, the magic required constant channelling, and the death of the caster - a near impossible feat as they maintained nigh-omniscience while in this plane - would collapse the plane and the death of all those housed within.

Though it has yet to be confirmed, some ancient texts indicate Nature Archmagisters were capable of a feat known as “soul eradication”, a technique that would completely destroy a soul by converting it into pure Nature Magic and infusing it into the plane around them. Although this information cannot be verified, the implications of such a dangerous form of magic raises questions about their pacifist nature and unwillingness to fight.

Compared to modern Nature Magisters - of which very little is known even today, given the nomadic and asocial nature of many - their magic seems to have changed with the inclusion of the Lattice. No longer does it focus on creation of the new - planes, soul energy, etc. - but rather on the recycling and symbiotic relationship they have with the existing planes they inhabit. Whether this was due to limitations imposed by the Lattice potentially filtering their magic as it does with other Magic types, loss of knowledge, or changes in the underlying planes they inhabit is unknown.

The Great Sundering

Where the Arcane Lattice came from, how it can repel even the gods themselves, what fuels it, and how it can be so absolute in its power is largely a mystery. It is the subject of many studies and experiments and continues to provide both large amounts of conflicting data, and no meaningful data at all. The only thing that is known with absolute certainty is that the Arcane Lattice first appeared during the cataclysmic event known as The Great Sundering. Several ancient reports from multiple sources - Divine, Arcane, and even seldom Nature - show clear understanding that directly after this event, magic was forever changed.

While it is still unknown what caused the Sundering - no records from the time show any understanding or indication of what could have happened - what is known is that it was a terrible and apocalyptic-level event that nearly completely erased all life across multiple planes of existence. Our greatest understanding is that while the Great War raged on, a sudden force unlike any other originated from the Material Plane and completely annihilated over 90% of all life. Trillions of individuals were killed in an instant, their bodies and souls wiped from realities in a split second, and large swathes of lands were scorched and desecrated by this unknown force, taking many hundreds of years to carefully nurture back to health.

Mere moments after the Sundering, Zhindoren - the single supercontinent of Qeenek - was torn into two distinct lands, Zyxs and Navirar. Other planes reported similar fractures such as lands being split apart (as occurred in the Elemental Plane, now split into four), ideologies being fragmented (such as the Feywild being split into more than just the Four Seasonal Courts), and understandings being faceted (as occurred in the Abyss, leading to several layers being split into new ones). The effect was instantaneous and devastating.

With such a terrible near-extinction event occurring, it is little wonder that the Great War was brought to a swift end as those fortunate enough to survive had little choice but to band together and work with each other to endure the tough years that followed. Although it was not always perfect, indeed many conflicts still arose around that time and festered for many centuries afterwards, with the Arcane Lattice in place the damage able to be caused was thankfully drastically mitigated and slowly but surely people were able to repopulate, grow, expand, and make the world and planes we live in today.
