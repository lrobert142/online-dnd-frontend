Realms of the Gods

Aetherus is the name of the plane of existence far in the outer realms that houses the gods worshipped by mortals and otherworldly creatures alike. A supposedly infinite expanse of chaos and order in constant combat, shift and change. It contains not just good, purity, and kindness (such as the kindly gods like Bahamut and Ilmater) but also evil, chaos, and death (such as the cruel gods Tiamat and Bhaal) and even aspects completely foreign and esoteric (such as the endless forests of Silvanus, or the temporal hourglass dunes of Chronos).

Many have attempted to explore and document this largely unknown plane, but very few have been granted admittance to any true aspect of it as the gods fiercely protect it and keep out would be scholars and invaders alike. While travelling to small partitions of it is possible - indeed, each god seems to holds a small segment of this infinite as their own personal realm from which they have complete control - travelling across these partitions seems to be nearly impossible unless travel is negotiated with the other god to which plane you wish to travel.

It has of yet not been confirmed if there are physical barriers between these partitions, or if indeed there is any unclaimed space between them at all. This has also made it impossible to determine if there is a certain 'order' to the realms that would constitute making a map, or if they are freely moving and changing similar to the Feywild.

To date, much is still unknown.
