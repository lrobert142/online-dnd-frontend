Spoilseed Oil: Its Dangers and You!

Warning: Do not let spoilseed touch any part of your skin, and immediately report it and its creator to the guards! Do not attempt to destroy it by yourself, you may end up infected!

Spoilseed oil is a thick, viscous, almost tar-like liquid that bubbles continuously even outside sources of intense heat and seems to almost move on its own. It is a terrible concoction brewed by the insane, the cruel, and the downright evil using ingredients so vile and methods so horrendous they will not be described in this missive. Any who dabble in making this highly illegal substance are to be considered an enemy of the state and must be immediately reported to the nearest guard.

Spoilseed Oil Infection

When this vile liquid makes contact with skin, it will immediately begin seeping into a victim's pores and infiltrating their bloodstream. At first it will be hardly recognisable and is often mistaken for a simple rash, presenting as only a slight red swelling of the infected area and minor itchiness. As the infection progresses, victims report dizziness, hearing distant voices, seeing dark figures in the corners of their vision and difficulty concentrating.

By 24 hours the infection has spread throughout the entire body and symptoms will also include nausea, difficulty making decisions, headaches, and extreme mood swings often leading to bouts of rage. At this point there are still no obvious visible signs of infection beyond the rash spreading to the arms and hands, and the itchiness often resulting in scratch-through of the afflicted areas.

Symptoms will gradually enhance over time as the victim becomes more and more unstable and irrational, and many unfortunate enough to have endured this cruelty have reported moments of "watching their bodies act on their own while trapped in their own mind".

After approximately 48 hours, the victim will have succumbed entirely to the spoilseed oil infection and will now begin displaying visible black veins along their arms and wrists. By this point the victim is no longer in control of their body and is completely under control of whomever created the concoction and will work tirelessly to enact whatever commands their terrible commander gives them.

During this time victims should be treated with extreme caution as they are no longer able to stop themselves from harming loved ones. Many have described heartbreaking stories of being made to watch as their body acts on its own to commit atrocities against friends and former allies.

Curing the Infection

Thankfully a cure to this horrible infection is available and can be made by any half-competent alchemist or healer! If you suspect you have been infected by spoilseed oil, you must immediately alert a guard and allow yourself to be detained so that you may be administered medical assistance at once. Any delays to this alerting may result in permanent and irreparable damage done to yourself, your loved ones, and your reputation as a law-abiding citizen.

Cures are typically administered via an injection to the site of the infection and a daily follow-up of anti-spoilseed potions taken daily for a tenday. Alternatively, if one has access to the funds, a healer able to administer a Greater Restoration spell can purge the infection from the body at once, without the need for additional medical assistance.

Addendum: Please note that any suggestions of this liquid being "alive" are completely nonsensical! Many alchemical guilds have gone on record stating that the ability for it to move on its own or communicate with the infected is factually incorrect and those who think otherwise must be reported to the nearest guard for questioning.
